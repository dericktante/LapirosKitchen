Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about getting Lapiros Kitchen delivered to you.

Shipping Options

We ship our authentic Cameroonian dishes anywhere in the United States. All orders are carefully packaged with ice packs to ensure freshness upon arrival.

Overnight Shipping

Order arrives the next business day. Best for perishable dishes. Pricing varies by location.

2-Day Express

Order arrives within 2 business days. Available for most locations across the US.

Local Pickup

Pick up your order directly from our kitchen at 12405 Coywood, Oklahoma City, OK 73142. Free of charge.

Packaging

Every shipped order is packed with care using insulated containers and ice packs designed to keep your food fresh and safe during transit. We take food safety seriously and only ship orders we are confident will arrive in perfect condition.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs vary based on your location, the size of your order, and the shipping method selected. To get an accurate quote for your order, please contact us directly:

Call (405) 227-3385

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, we will send you a tracking number via phone or email so you can monitor your delivery in real time.

Questions?

If you have any questions about shipping, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
