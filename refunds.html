Policies

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. Here's how we handle refunds and cancellations.

Our Commitment

At Lapiros Kitchen, we take pride in every dish we prepare. Your satisfaction means everything to us. If something isn't right with your order, we want to make it right.

Refund Policy

We offer a full refund within 24 hours of receiving your order under the following conditions:

  • The order arrived damaged or spoiled during shipping
  • The wrong items were included in your order
  • The order did not meet the quality standards we promise

To request a refund, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order with a description of the issue. We may ask for a photo of the item in question.

Cancellations

Need to cancel your order? No problem — just reach out to us directly and we'll take care of it. Since all our dishes are made fresh to order, we kindly ask that you contact us as early as possible.

To cancel your order (calls only):

Calls only: (405) 227-3385

Non-Refundable Situations

We are unable to offer refunds in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after the order has been prepared
  • Incorrect delivery address provided by the customer
  • Refund request made more than 24 hours after delivery

Questions?

If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please don't hesitate to reach out. We're always happy to help.
